Golden Palace - Felicity Solar

↗

**Golden Palace-Felicity Solar** is a smart energy solutions platform that helps users explore products, view detailed specifications, and submit orders. It also includes an interactive energy consumption calculator that recommends suitable batteries, solar panels, and inverters based on household or office usage. Additionally, users can diagnose technical issues, follow self-troubleshooting steps, contact technical support, request maintenance services, and manage product warranties.

Telegram Bot

↗

Telegram Game Recharge Bot is an automated Telegram bot for purchasing and delivering game top-up packages. It integrates with the **Sham Cash API** to process and verify payments, automatically assigns user roles based on their permissions, and displays available game packages with their images and details. The bot streamlines the entire recharge process from package selection and payment to transaction verification and order completion.

Python Html JavaScript API
